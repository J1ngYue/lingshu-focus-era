# 修仙角色 v2

使用内置 imagegen 生成透明 PNG。原图直接保存为 `cultivators-v2.png`，未重绘、抠图或修改像素。`pack-cultivators.py` 只读取透明边界，输出九个角色姿态的采样坐标；游戏直接从原图取样并按同一角色尺度、脚底基线绘制。

三列依次为青白剑修、冰蓝剑修、紫衣符修，三行依次为待机、出剑、受击。佩剑由装备系统独立绘制，品质影响剑身和灵光，避免美术中固定武器与实际装备重叠。

## 最终提示词

+Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one transparent 2D xianxia game character animation sprite atlas, THREE columns by THREE rows, nine isolated full-body sprites, wide 4:3 canvas.
Primary request: replace ugly block-faced chibi heroes with beautiful Chinese cultivation fantasy sword heroes for a landscape idle learning game. Professional Chinese manhua / anime game sprites, charming compact proportions about 3.5 heads tall, natural almond eyes, handsome delicate faces, nuanced cel shading, crisp fine dark outlines, flowing layered hanfu, long black hair, cloth ribbons and jade ornaments. Genuine transparent background.
Layout: exactly 3 columns and 3 rows. Each sprite centered in its own equal cell; entire head, hair, sleeves and feet contained inside the cell, generous transparent padding between cells, feet aligned within each row. Facing screen RIGHT in three-quarter side view. All sprites use the same scale and perspective.
Column 1 identity: handsome young male sword cultivator, long black half-up ponytail and jade hair crown, ivory and muted jade-green hanfu, slim layered dark belt, elegant long robe split for movement, black boots.
Column 2 identity: male advanced sword cultivator, black topknot with flowing locks, ice blue and white martial dao robes, silver trim, slightly more armored shoulders, black boots.
Column 3 identity: female spell-and-sword cultivator, long black hair and lilac hair ribbon, lavender and ivory hanfu, delicate jade ornaments, purple waist sash, black boots.
Row 1: idle combat stance, right hand closed naturally ready to grip an externally rendered sword, hand near right waist, left hand forming a small cultivation seal. Calm alert expression.
Row 2: attacking pose with right hand thrust forward at waist height, feet braced, flowing sleeves and hair swept back, intent expression.
Row 3: hit reaction with shoulders recoiling backward, robe and hair displaced, right hand still near right waist, startled pained expression.
Constraints: weapon blades are rendered separately by the game, so do NOT draw weapons, swords, bows, staffs, magic glows, particles, circles or objects in hands. Hands must be anatomically readable, each closed right hand clearly visible and free from sleeves. No environment, no ground shadows, no borders, no grid lines, no text, no watermark, no checkerboard painted into the image. Consistent character identity and costume between the three poses. Avoid huge square heads, rectangular faces, stick eyes, Western knight helmets, red hair, crossbows, pixel art, flat geometric placeholders, realistic photos and 3D renders.
